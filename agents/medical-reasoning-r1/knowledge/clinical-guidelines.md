# Clinical Guidelines — Evidence-Based Anchors and How to Use Them

Guidelines are **priors, not verdicts**. Use conventional threshold numbers to
structure reasoning, always name the convention you're citing, and override
with patient-specific factors explicitly.

## How to cite

- State threshold + source convention ("per typical ESC/ACC-style thresholds",
  "common pediatric fever convention") — never fabricate a study or exact
  citation. If the user needs the primary guideline, tell them which body to
  check (ACC/AHA, ESC, NICE, WHO, IDSA, ACOG, ADA, GOLD, KDIGO, local protocol).
- Distinguish **screening**, **diagnosis**, and **treatment-target** thresholds —
  mixing them is the classic error.
- When local protocols plausibly differ (sepsis bundles, pediatric febrile
  infant workups, anticoagulation pathways), say so and follow the user's
  local system.

## Common anchors (verify against current local guidelines)

### Cardiovascular
- Hypertension: diagnosis typically ≥130/80 (ACC/AHA-style) or ≥140/90
  (ESC-style) on repeated properly-measured readings; confirm with ABPM/HBPM
  before labeling. Targets commonly <130/80 for most adults, individualized
  (elderly, frailty, CAD, CKD).
- Chest pain risk: HEART score for undifferentiated ACS in the ED; ECG within
  10 minutes for concerning presentations; troponin at 0/1h or 0/2h protocols.
- Anticoagulation in AF: CHA₂DS₂-VASc drives decision; HAS-BLED for bleeding
  risk (mitigate, don't just withhold). DOACs first-line except mechanical
  valves/moderate-severe mitral stenosis (warfarin).
- VTE: Wells or PERC for PE; Wells + D-dimer for DVT; D-dimer threshold
  age-adjusted (typically age×10 ng/mL FEU in patients >50). Anticoagulate
  while imaging pending only when pretest is moderate-high.

### Infection & sepsis
- Sepsis: suspected infection + organ dysfunction (qSOFA ≥2 is a screen, not
  the definition); lactate, cultures before antibiotics when it doesn't
  delay, broad-spectrum antibiotics within 1 hour for septic shock.
- Antibiotics: document the syndrome, the likely organisms, local resistance,
  and the de-escalation/culture review plan. Duration: shortest effective
  course unless source/organism demands otherwise.
- Febrile infant <90 days: full risk-stratified workup per pediatric
  convention (UA/urine culture, blood culture, CBC ± CRP, LP by age/risk) —
  treat as a protocol-driven emergency pathway.
- Community-acquired pneumonia: severity score (e.g. CURB-65) drives
  outpatient vs inpatient; cover typical + atypical when indicated.

### Metabolic & endocrine
- Diabetes: A1c target ~7% for most, individualized (looser with frailty,
  hypoglycemia risk, short life expectancy). DKA: insulin infusion + fluids +
  potassium replacement protocol; never bolus insulin without checking K.
- Hyperkalemia: ECG changes = emergency; shift/excrete/remove strategy with
  the three-column plan; recheck K at defined intervals.
- Thyroid: TSH first-line for most; interpret with context (sick euthyroid,
  biotin interference, pregnancy-specific ranges).

### Pulmonary
- COPD/asthma: severity by symptoms + exacerbation history; step-up/step-down
  therapy; never rely on SABA without controller assessment; peak flow/spirometry
  interpretation by personal best where available.
- Respiratory failure: ABG interpretation with compensation rules; escalate
  care for rising CO₂ with acidosis, not just the number.

### Hematology & oncology support
- Anemia workup: MCV-split algorithm (micro = iron/BThal/chronic disease;
  macro = B12/folate/liver/thyroid/drugs; normo = bleed/hemolysis/chronic
  disease/renal); reticulocyte count or hemolysis labs to split further.
- Neutropenic fever: emergency pathway — cultures, empiric antipseudomonal
  β-lactam within 60 minutes, risk-stratify (MASCC/CISNE) for outpatient
  vs inpatient.

### Perioperative & prophylaxis
- Thromboprophylaxis: risk-stratify (surgical, medical, cancer, prior VTE);
  mechanical + pharmacologic per setting; dose-adjust for renal/weight.
- Statin decisions: 10-year risk categories per local guideline + risk
  enhancers; LDL targets by risk tier (e.g. <70 mg/dL for very high risk).

### Pain & palliative
- Multimodal analgesia first; opioids only with indication, bowel regimen,
  smallest effective dose, and a stop plan. Rotate/avoid stacking sedatives.
- Palliative: clarify goals-of-care before escalation discussions; symptom
  protocols (dyspnea, nausea, secretions, delirium) beat investigations when
  the goal is comfort.

## Uncertainty handling

- Label every number: **convention** (varies by body/local protocol),
  **patient-specific** (measured), or **assumed** (must be flagged).
- When two guidelines conflict, present both and the tie-breaker logic
  (patient values, comorbidity, local pathway).
- When the user's data is incomplete for a threshold decision (e.g. no ECG
  for HEART score), say exactly what's missing and what it would change.
- If the request would benefit from specialist input (oncology dosing,
  complex anticoagulation, psychiatric medication management in pregnancy),
  recommend it rather than simulating certainty.
